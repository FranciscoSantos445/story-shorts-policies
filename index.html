Story Shorts Automation

Home · Privacy Policy · Terms of Service

Story Shorts Automation turns publicly posted text stories into short vertical videos and publishes them to the developer's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. Every video has AI voice narration, word-by-word captions and background gameplay footage.

How it works

  1. Find a story. The tool collects publicly posted text stories from public web listings.
  2. Write the script. The story is cleaned up and trimmed to fit a short video, usually 30 to 90 seconds.
  3. Narrate. A text-to-speech voice reads the script aloud.
  4. Edit the video. The narration is combined with background footage and word-by-word captions, then rendered as a 1080×1920 vertical video.
  5. Write the caption. A caption and hashtags are generated from the story.
  6. Publish. The video is uploaded automatically to the developer's own accounts through each platform's official API.

TikTok integration

The app uses TikTok Login Kit to connect the developer's TikTok account and the Content Posting API to publish finished videos to it.

  • Scopes requested: user.info.basic and video.publish.
  • Before each post, the app queries TikTok's creator info and only uses a privacy level that TikTok allows for the account. Comments, Duet and Stitch are left enabled.
  • Videos are posted only to the account that authorized the app. That account's ID is saved at login and checked before every upload, and the upload is stopped if it doesn't match.
  • The app does not read followers, messages, analytics, or any other user's data.

YouTube integration

The app requests the youtube.upload permission only to upload the finished videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google user data.

Data and security

Access tokens are stored only on the developer's own computer and are sent only to the platform that issued them. Nothing is sold or shared. For full details, read the Privacy Policy.

Who can use it

This is a personal tool for a single developer. Only that developer's own accounts are connected, and there is no public sign-up.
