Story Shorts Automation

Story Shorts Automation is a personal-use tool run by a single developer. It turns publicly posted text stories into short vertical videos (AI voice narration, word-by-word captions and gameplay footage) and publishes them to the developer's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts.

How it uses Google / YouTube data

The app requests the youtube.upload permission only to upload the generated videos to the developer's own YouTube channel. It does not read, store or share any other YouTube or Google user data. Access tokens are stored locally on the developer's computer.

Who can use it

Only the developer's own accounts are connected. There is no public sign-up and no other users.
