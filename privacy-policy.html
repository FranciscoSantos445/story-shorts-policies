Privacy Policy

App: Story Shorts Automation
Last updated: 2026-09-29

This application is operated for personal use by a single individual developer. It does not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to any third party.

Data the app accesses

  • TikTok account data: the app uses the TikTok Content Posting API solely to upload videos to the developer's own connected TikTok account. It does not read, collect, or share data about any other TikTok user.
  • Publicly available text: the app sources publicly posted text content (e.g. from public web listings) to generate video narration. No personal data is extracted from this content beyond what the original author already published publicly.

Data storage

Authentication tokens issued by TikTok are stored locally on the developer's own machine and are not transmitted to any third party or server other than TikTok's own API.

Data sharing

No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
